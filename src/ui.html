How it works

  1. See. We read NASA satellite fire detections for Punjab and Haryana and score every ~5 km area for burn risk.
  2. Match. For the riskiest areas we pick the Happy Seeder that can get there soonest.
  3. Approve. The officer approves with one tap. The farmer and the machine operator are messaged.
  4. Confirm. The operator accepts, or says they can't, and the next machine is assigned. They tap Done after the visit.
  5. Check. The next day we look for fires in that area. If one is detected, the officer is alerted by email and Telegram.
